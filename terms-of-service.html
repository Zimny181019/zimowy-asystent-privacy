Zimowy Guard now operates as a single bot combining its original community and server-protection features with the previously separate partnership system and ticket system. By using Zimowy Guard on any Discord server, you agree to the terms below. If you do not agree, please discontinue use of the bot.

1️⃣ Nature of the Service

Zimowy Guard is a Discord bot that combines community engagement features — slash commands, member verification, a riddle/quiz voting system, self-service color roles, and a reputation system — with server protection features, including moderation tools, anti-raid and anti-nuke systems, automated anti-spam detection, and server/user blacklisting; a set of community/utility features: sticky messages, a "Guess the Number" minigame with role rewards and a win leaderboard, a birthday celebration system, and invite tracking; a partnership system, which lets designated "realizators" register partnerships (invite exchanges) with other servers, enforces an activity quota for realizators, and automatically ends partnerships when a partner leaves a server; and a ticket system, which allows users to open private ticket channels with server staff — including for partnership-related requests, advertising, payouts, and rewards — and generates a transcript of the conversation when a ticket is closed. The bot operates exclusively within the Discord server(s) on which it is installed.

2️⃣ Member Verification

  • ✅ Clicking the verification button grants you a designated role on the server, used to confirm you have completed the verification process.
  • ⚠️ If the verification role has been removed or misconfigured on a given server, verification through the bot may temporarily fail until server staff resolve it.

3️⃣ Riddles & Quizzes

  • 🧩 Each riddle/quiz allows one vote per user. Once submitted, a vote is final and cannot be changed.
  • 📊 Aggregated vote counts are displayed publicly and update automatically as users vote.
  • 🔒 Voting closes once a riddle is marked as finished by the bot; no further votes are accepted afterward.

4️⃣ Color Roles & Reputation

Users may add or remove available color roles from the color-selection menu at any time, for cosmetic purposes only. Color roles carry no permissions beyond the visual display name color. The reputation system (+rep / ?rep) lets members award each other points in a designated channel; reputation totals are cosmetic and carry no server permissions.

5️⃣ Vacation / Leave Mode ("Urlop")

Members or staff may request a temporary "leave" ("urlop") period during which their roles are stored and hidden, and automatically restored once the leave period ends or they end it early. This feature is intended for genuine, temporary absences and may be reversed early by staff if misused. Where applicable, holding an approved leave role also pauses the realizator activity quota (see Section 8) and restricts visibility into open ticket channels (see Section 10) for the duration of the leave.

6️⃣ Permission-Gated Commands

Some slash commands require specific Discord permissions within the channel they are used in. If you lack the required permissions, the command will be blocked and you will be notified accordingly.

🤝 7️⃣ Partnership System

Designated "realizators" may register partnerships (invite exchanges) with other Discord servers through a designated channel. The following limits and restrictions apply:

  • 🔁 A maximum of 2 partnerships may be registered with the same partner within a 7-day period.
  • 🪞 A 2-day cooldown applies between partnerships registered by a realizator involving themselves.
  • 🚫 Servers appearing on the shared blacklist (maintained by a separate blacklist bot) cannot be partnered with.
  • 📎 Only valid Discord invite links (discord.gg / discord.com/invite) submitted in the designated channel are accepted for a partnership.
  • 🚪 A partnership is automatically ended and removed from records when the partner leaves the associated server.

⏱️ 8️⃣ Realizator Norm (Activity Quota)

  • ⏱️ Members holding the realizator role must complete a set number of partnerships within each 2-day cycle.
  • 📩 A reminder is sent by DM after 1 day if the quota has not yet been met.
  • 🚫 If the quota is not met by the end of the cycle, the realizator role is automatically removed.
  • 🌴 The norm does not apply while a realizator holds an approved leave role; it resumes with a fresh cycle once leave ends.

🎁 9️⃣ Reward Points

  • 🎁 Once a realizator reaches a set number of completed partnerships, they may open a payout ticket via the ticket system. Server staff then manually credit the corresponding virtual points to that realizator's balance via the ZR Points bot — Zimowy Guard does not process or facilitate any monetary payment.
  • 🚫 Points have no monetary value and cannot be exchanged for real currency or assets outside the server.
  • 📊 The rate of points assigned per partnership may differ for a brand-new partnership versus a renewed one (the same partner/server pairing after the cooldown period).
  • ⚖️ Server staff reserve the right to correct point balances or partnership counts in the event of system errors, abuse, or exploits.
  • 📄 Use of awarded points is additionally governed by the ZR Points bot's own Terms of Service.

🎫 🔟 Ticket System

  • 🎫 Ticket channels are created on a per-user basis and are only visible to the ticket owner and the staff roles configured for that ticket type (including partnership requests, advertising purchases, payouts, and rewards).
  • 🙋‍♂️ Staff may "claim" a ticket to take ownership of it, and "release" it back if needed, which adjusts channel permissions accordingly.
  • 🔒 Tickets may be closed by authorized staff, or automatically if the ticket owner leaves the server. Closed ticket channels are deleted.
  • 📄 A transcript of the ticket conversation is generated when the ticket is closed and delivered to the ticket owner, the staff member who closed it, and a staff log channel, as described in the Privacy Policy.

🛡️ 1️⃣1️⃣ Moderation & Server Protection

  • 📜 Moderation actions taken by staff (kicks, bans, warns, mutes, deleted messages) may be logged, along with the reason and the staff member responsible, for accountability purposes.
  • 🚨 Anti-raid protection may temporarily lock down the server (e.g. slow verification, restrict invites) if an unusually large number of members join in a short period.
  • 🛑 Anti-nuke protection automatically responds to suspicious, potentially destructive admin-level actions (such as mass channel/role deletion, unauthorized bot additions, or server icon/emoji changes) by removing dangerous permissions, and — depending on server configuration — may kick or ban the account responsible.
  • 🚫 Automated anti-spam detection may act on accounts that post messages at an abnormal rate or in a repetitive/flooding pattern.
  • ⛔ The bot owner/staff may add servers or users to an internal blacklist (e.g. to deny partnership requests or restrict access) for rule violations or abuse.
Automated security actions are designed to protect the server and its members. Where a false positive occurs, server staff or the bot owner may reverse the action.

🎮 1️⃣2️⃣ Guessing Game & Reward Roles

  • 🎮 Reward roles from the "Guess the Number" game are awarded randomly based on configured odds — they cannot be purchased and have no monetary value.
  • 🏆 Win counts and leaderboard standings reflect in-game activity only and may be adjusted by server staff in the event of errors or abuse.
  • 🔄 The ranking may be reset or corrected by administrators at their discretion.

🎂 1️⃣3️⃣ Birthdays & Invite Tracking

  • 🎂 Birthday entries may require staff approval before being activated, and are used solely to trigger an automated announcement (and, where configured, a temporary role) on the member's birthday.
  • 📥 Invite tracking records which invite was used to bring a member into the server, for moderation and community-management purposes (e.g. invite rewards, spotting abuse of the invite system).
