1️⃣ Nature of the Service

Zimowy Guard is a Discord bot that combines community engagement features — slash commands, member verification, a riddle/quiz voting system, self-service color roles, and a reputation system — with server protection features, including moderation tools, anti-raid and anti-nuke systems, and server/user blacklisting. The bot operates exclusively within the Discord server(s) on which it is installed.

2️⃣ Member Verification

  • ✅ Clicking the verification button grants you a designated role on the server, used to confirm you have completed the verification process.
  • ⚠️ If the verification role has been removed or misconfigured on a given server, verification through the bot may temporarily fail until server staff resolve it.

3️⃣ Riddles & Quizzes

  • 🧩 Each riddle/quiz allows one vote per user. Once submitted, a vote is final and cannot be changed.
  • 📊 Aggregated vote counts are displayed publicly and update automatically as users vote.
  • 🔒 Voting closes once a riddle is marked as finished by the bot; no further votes are accepted afterward.

4️⃣ Color Roles & Reputation

Users may add or remove available color roles from the color-selection menu at any time, for cosmetic purposes only. Color roles carry no permissions beyond the visual display name color. The reputation system (+rep / ?rep) lets members award each other points in a designated channel; reputation totals are cosmetic and carry no server permissions.

5️⃣ Vacation / Leave Mode

Members or staff may request a temporary "leave" period during which their roles are stored and hidden, and automatically restored once the leave period ends or they end it early. This feature is intended for genuine, temporary absences and may be reversed early by staff if misused.

6️⃣ Permission-Gated Commands

Some slash commands require specific Discord permissions within the channel they are used in. If you lack the required permissions, the command will be blocked and you will be notified accordingly.
